sci-fi audio speaker or sound beacon with three restrained violet Ether-wave
arcs, compact symmetrical silhouette, centered and front-facing, readable at

Premium contemporary game UI asset, clean cel-shaded 3D illustration, not
pixel art.

the icon itself contains no green. Keep the chroma source unchanged under
`../chroma/system-icon-g18-v1-chroma.png`; the alpha master is generated at
